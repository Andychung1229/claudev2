A contact is two residues within 8 angstroms, at least two apart in sequence. We count contacts and opportunities for every amino-acid pair across all frames, so f(a,b) is how often that pair type is in contact. We also keep the mean contact frequency for each separation band, p(d). Notice the scale: residue type changes f by about 2 times, but separation changes p(d) by about 1,300 times. This matters for how we rank edges, which we come back to later.
