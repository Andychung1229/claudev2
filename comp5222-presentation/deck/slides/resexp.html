[~1:00] Here are 151 sequences with experimentally measured Rg. The model never saw an experimental label, yet it tracks them with a correlation of 0.92. Here the graph model does beat the composition model: 0.295 versus 0.331 nanometres, and a paired bootstrap puts that difference clearly below zero. Twenty-two of these sequences, the orange diamonds, sit inside training sequences; dropping them changes nothing. The caveat is that the error is three times larger than on simulations. Our labels come from one simulation model, so we inherit its gap to experiment. So why does the graph help so little on simulations? We looked closely at the edges.
