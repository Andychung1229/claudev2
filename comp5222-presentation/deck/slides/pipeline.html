COMP 5222 · ContactGAT

Two halves. Offline, once: we scan all Human IDRome trajectories and count residue contacts within 8 Å, then pool them into a 20 by 20 table by amino-acid pair. Online, per sequence: we look up that table to build a residue graph, run two edge-aware GAT layers, pool, and output Rg. At inference we never need a trajectory for the new protein.
