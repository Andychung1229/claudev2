COMP 5222 · Project presentation

Contact-frequency graph attention for predicting the radius of gyration of intrinsically disordered proteins

Turning simulation contact statistics into a residue graph, and predicting protein size with an 18.7k-parameter graph attention network

Tian Jiaxin · Chung Ka Wai

[~0:25] Good afternoon, everyone. We are Tian Jiaxin and Chung Ka Wai. Our project asks one question: can we predict how big a disordered protein is, just from its sequence, by treating that sequence as a graph? In the next twelve minutes we will cover the biology first, then how we turned simulation data into a graph, the model we trained on it, and finally what the results tell us — including what we still need to fix.
