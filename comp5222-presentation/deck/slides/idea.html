[~0:45] Here is our idea in one picture. Instead of asking the network to rediscover which residue pairs matter, we hand it that information as a graph. Steps one and two happen once, offline. We take the Human IDRome simulation trajectories and count how often each type of amino-acid pair comes into contact. That gives us a small contact table. Steps three and four run for every sequence. We turn the sequence into a residue graph whose edges come from that table, and a small graph attention network reads the graph and predicts Rg.
