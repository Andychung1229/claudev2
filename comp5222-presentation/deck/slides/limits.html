[~0:55] Before we finish, here is what we still need to fix. We think it is important to be upfront about these. One: our contact table was counted over all twenty-eight thousand trajectories, including the validation and test chains. It is only a twenty-by-twenty summary, so the leak is small, but our proposal said training data only, and we will rebuild it that way. Two: the table has no distance bands. Because the pair frequency is the same at every distance, dividing by the average for each band mostly favours the farthest residues — that is why the orange edges sat far from the diagonal earlier. The amino-acid type only decides among those far residues. A band-resolved table, as in our proposal, should fix this. Three: we have run only the length-only baseline so far, with one seed. The controlled comparisons — a local-only graph, a graph with random edges, and fixed instead of learned attention — are next, with three seeds. Four: we have not yet tested which edges the model actually relies on. We plan edge-removal experiments for that.
