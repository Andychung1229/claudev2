[~1:20] On four thousand unseen proteins, the model is accurate: mean error 0.10 nanometres, R squared 0.98. But a high R squared here proves very little, because length alone already gives 0.97. So we tested two simple baselines on the same split. Against length alone, our model cuts the error by about a fifth. The harder test is a linear model on amino-acid composition plus length: just 21 parameters. And on simulated data, it ties with our graph model: 0.098 versus 0.100 nanometres, and the difference is within noise. Of the variation that length cannot explain, our model captures 36 percent and the linear model 31. So on this benchmark, composition and length explain most of what our model predicts. We think any IDP predictor should report this baseline. Simulations are one thing. What about real experiments?
