[~1:15] Here is the simulated test set: four thousand proteins the model never saw. The grey bars are two simple baselines. Length alone gives 0.124 nanometres. Adding amino-acid composition, with just 21 parameters, gives 0.098. Our full model, with the contact edges, averages 0.111 over three seeds. That is no better than composition. Our earlier single run gave 0.100, but it did not reproduce on retraining, so we report the three-seed mean. Now the orange bar. Same network, same training, but we remove every non-local edge and keep only the chain. The error drops to 0.066, and all three seeds beat composition. It explains about two thirds of what length cannot explain, against one third for composition. So message passing along the chain works. Our contact edges are what hold it back.
