Step 3 · Model

Edge-aware attention, then a length-aware pool

1 · Score each edge

α_ij = softmax_j LeakyReLU(a_s·Wh_i + a_t·Wh_j + a_e·φ(e_ij))

Softmax runs only over the residue’s graph neighbours (edge mask).

2 · Update each residue

h_i ← ELU(LayerNorm(h_i + Σ_j α_ij · W h_j))

4 heads × 16 channels = 64; 2 layers; dropout 0.2.

3 · Pool the chain

ln R̂g = MLP([mean_i h_i ‖ max_i h_i ‖ log N])

log N keeps chain length visible to the regression head.

contact_mpnn/model.py · configs/sparse_gat.yaml

Each GAT layer scores an edge from the two node states plus the embedded edge features, so the contact prior enters the attention logit. Softmax runs only over graph neighbours. The update is a residual connection with LayerNorm and ELU. After two layers we take the mean and max over residues and append log N, then a small MLP predicts ln Rg. The whole model has under 19 thousand parameters and trains in about 13 minutes.
