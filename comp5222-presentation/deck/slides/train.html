[~0:35] We split the data seventy, fifteen, fifteen by protein. Regions from the same protein, and identical sequences, stay in one split, so test proteins are truly unseen. That gives about nineteen thousand training chains and four thousand each for validation and test. We train on log Rg and keep the checkpoint with the lowest validation error. Because single runs turned out to be noisy, we trained every model with three random seeds. So, how well does it do?
